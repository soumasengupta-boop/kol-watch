This week

Novartis' topline press release says ORION-4, the first dedicated inclisiran CVOT to read out, reduced cardiovascular events. Kausik Ray and Erin Michos reacted to it.

ORION-4 topline drives LDL conversation

Erin Michos called it the first dedicated inclisiran CVOT to read out. She said it reduced CV events and again confirmed the value of lowering LDL-C. Kausik Ray shared the Novartis press release on the topline. He said he began working on inclisiran development over a decade ago and that it is now used by 1.2 million people. Treat his post as industry-adjacent, even though he is not flagged as industry. No effect sizes were shared.

Michos ↗Ray ↗
Topol: post-MI flu vaccine is Class 1A but underused

Eric Topol posted on LinkedIn and Bluesky that several randomized trials show a flu shot after MI reduces mortality and other adverse CV outcomes. He added that a new Swedish nationwide registry report backs this up. He noted the shot is Class 1, Level A in the European and American guidelines but is not implemented in practice. The posts were the week's most engaged, at 718 on LinkedIn and 380 on Bluesky.

Topol ↗Topol ↗
VESALIUS-CV: evolocumab benefit in reduced eGFR

C. Michael Gibson shared a video discussion with Jan Cornel on VESALIUS-CV. In patients with reduced eGFR, evolocumab lowered 3-point MACE by 39% and 4-point MACE by 23%, with NNTs of 20–25. Gibson framed the result as intensive LDL-C lowering reducing risk in impaired kidney function.

Gibson ↗
Interventional trial flow, mostly via JACC

JACC posts covered four interventional/imaging items. NATURE (156 pts) found stent-retriever thrombectomy before PCI reduced infarct size on CMR in STEMI with large thrombus burden. In FUSION (528 pts), FFR-CT led to fewer angiographies showing no obstructive disease, 18% vs 33%. VICTORY found super-high-pressure noncompliant balloons noninferior to intravascular lithotripsy for stent expansion. A meta-analysis of 28 RCTs (24,634 pts) found IVUS/OCT beat angiography for target lesion failure, MI, stent thrombosis and mortality. Deepak Bhatt also highlighted DAPT-SHOCK-AMI, with the main results in The Lancet and an imaging substudy in Circulation.

Ray on HORIZON and Lp(a)

Kausik Ray posted twice on the HORIZON trial. He pointed to an EAS statement published the previous week on the trial and its implications for other therapies and testing. He also posted his own thoughts on HORIZON and Lp(a). The posts gave no data. Both drew low engagement (52 and 20).

SCAD review and LIFECARD CKM milestone

Eric Topol highlighted a Lancet review of spontaneous coronary artery dissection (SCAD), by Jacqueline Saw and Peter Clemmensen. He said it causes heart attacks in about 25% of women under 50. Salim Virani reported that LIFECARD had completed 1,600 FibroScan assessments. The study is expanding its cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic health assessments.
